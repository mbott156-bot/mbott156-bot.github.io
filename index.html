Case studies

Three projects, in brief

Turtl

From content platform to intent data platform

Starting point
Turtl began as a way to publish and view documents online. Useful, but hard to tie to revenue.
What changed
I helped lead the shift to tracking how each reader engaged with content and mapping that activity straight into the CRM.
Result
Customers gained first-party intent data their sales teams could act on, and Turtl was repositioned as an intent data platform.
The Customization Group

Go-to-market for a new corporate division

What changed
I wrote the full go-to-market strategy, implemented HubSpot for the GCC, and led marketing attribution and reporting.
Result
The division launched into key enterprise markets with reporting that tied campaigns to pipeline from day one.
Nestlé Professional

Turning sales and marketing data-driven
